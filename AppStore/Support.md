# Trackpad Music support

Choose **Open Audio…** and select a local audio file such as an MP3, M4A, WAV, or AIFF. Click **Play** or press Space. Drag the playback slider to move through the track.

The **Live audio** section shows the waveform and separates bass, midrange, and highs. Turn on **Reactive bass synth** and use **Synth mix** to adjust the extra bass layer. The **Speakers** switch mutes both the song and synth.

If a file does not play, try a local, unprotected WAV or MP3. Wait for the audio analysis to finish before expecting the visualizer or synth to respond.
