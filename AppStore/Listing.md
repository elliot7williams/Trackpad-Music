## Promotional text

See your music as it plays. Follow the waveform and bass levels live, then blend in a reactive synth that follows your song.

## Description

Watch your music move. Trackpad Music plays audio files on your Mac and shows the waveform as the song plays, with separate meters for bass, midrange, and highs.

Turn on Reactive Bass Synth to add a layer that follows the song’s bass pitch and intensity. Use Synth Mix to dial it in, then pause or scrub through the track while the synth follows playback.

- Open local audio files such as MP3, M4A, WAV, and AIFF.
- See a live waveform and three frequency meters.
- Blend in an optional bass synth that reacts to the track.
- Play and analyze audio entirely on your Mac.

## What’s new
