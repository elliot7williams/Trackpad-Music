# Trackpad Music privacy policy

Trackpad Music does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app contains no account system, advertising, or analytics.

When you open Music Library and allow access, the app reads song information from the Mac’s Music library to display the list. It opens audio files you select from that list or through the macOS file picker. It analyzes those files on your Mac to show the waveform and frequency levels and to generate the optional bass synth. The original files are left as they are. Temporary synth audio is kept locally and is removed when replaced or when macOS clears temporary files. Trackpad Music does not send local audio or library information to its own servers. When you choose to play a cloud song, Apple’s MusicKit service handles the library and playback requests using your Apple account.

For questions about this policy, use the contact listed on the Trackpad Music support page.
