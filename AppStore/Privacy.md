# Trackpad Music privacy policy

Trackpad Music does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app contains no account system, advertising, or analytics.

The app accesses audio files you choose through the macOS file picker. It analyzes those files on your Mac to show the waveform and frequency levels and to generate the optional bass synth. The original files are left as they are. Temporary synth audio is kept locally and is removed when replaced or when macOS clears temporary files. No audio is uploaded.

For questions about this policy, use the contact listed on the Trackpad Music support page.
